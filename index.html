Chapter 1 - At Home

At Home

Yoru: "Kaito, why are you so sad these days?"

*He doesn't know how it feels to lose someone...*

Kaito looked away and said, "You will understand someday. Now I should go, my team is waiting."

.....Kaito went to meet his team.....

Near a Lake Beside the Arena

*Ever since Echo died, Kaito has been sad...* Haru thought.

"Are you still thinking about Echo?" Haru asked with slight anger on her face.

Kaito stayed silent for a moment before replying, "I'm not. Why should I? He was my friend, but thinking about the past and regretting it is just a waste of time."

....Kaito didn't know what was coming after his friend's death....

...Kaito and Haru went to the academy to watch the selection exam for their siblings. It was a fighting examination held every year for the selection of students...

In the Arena

The arena was crowded with families cheering for their children....

"I'm scared, brother." Yoru said hesitantly.

"Who's your opponent?" Kaito asked while looking into Yoru's eyes.

"My friend, Sora." Yoru replied.

"I understand how you feel. Just be confident and remember that you're as strong as your opponent. For now, he's not your friend. Analyze every move your opponent makes, and your own as well." Kaito said while entering the arena.

The exam began.

"Welcome to this year's Academy Selection Exam!" the announcer's voice echoed across the arena.

"A total of one hundred and twenty students are participating today. The rules are simple: students may fight using martial arts, weapons, or abilities approved by the academy. A match ends if a participant surrenders, loses consciousness, or is unable to continue fighting. Killing is strictly forbidden."

"Only the strongest and smartest students will be selected. Show us your strength!"

Before the junior matches began, several senior students entered the arena to demonstrate their abilities...

The audience watched in amazement as flames burst across the battlefield, swords clashed at incredible speed, and shockwaves cracked the arena floor.

Some students defeated their opponents with pure strength, while others relied on speed and strategy.

Yoru stared silently at the battlefield while Kaito watched the seniors with sharp eyes...

The crowd roared with excitement as students entered the battlefield one by one...

"First match! Yoru versus Sora!" the announcer shouted.

Yoru slowly walked into the arena while clenching his trembling hands.

*I can't lose... not in front of brother...*

Across the arena, Sora smiled confidently.

"Don't hold back, Yoru." Sora said while taking his stance.

Kaito watched silently from above while Haru stood beside him.

"The match begins!"

Sora rushed forward first, throwing a fast punch toward Yoru's face.

BAM!

Yoru barely blocked the attack and stepped backwards.

"Too slow!" Sora shouted before attacking again.

Sora launched multiple attacks without stopping. Punches and kicks came one after another like a storm.

The audience cheered loudly.

Yoru remembered Kaito's words.

"Analyze every move of your opponent..."

Yoru narrowed his eyes and carefully focused on Sora's movements.

Sora charged once more.

"This will finish it!"

As Sora threw a powerful punch, Yoru suddenly dodged sideways.

"What?!"

Yoru quickly struck Sora in the stomach.

THUD!

Sora stumbled backwards in shock.

The crowd gasped.

"Yoru landed a hit!"

Kaito's eyes widened slightly.

*He figured out the pattern already...?*

Sora wiped the blood from the corner of his mouth and smiled.

"Looks like you've gotten stronger."

Yoru finally took a fighting stance confidently.

"I'm not losing."

The battle had only just begun...

END OF CHAPTER 1

Chapter 2 - In Arena

Yoru took a fighting stance confidently while staring directly at Sora.

"You can do this!" Kaito cheered from the audience.

Sora suddenly dashed forward and launched a powerful kick toward Yoru's head. Yoru quickly blocked the attack and countered with a heavy punch to Sora's chest.

The impact echoed throughout the arena as both fighters continued exchanging fast and brutal attacks. Blood dripped from their injuries while the crowd roared loudly at the intense battle.

After a long and exhausting fight, Yoru gathered the last of his strength and threw one final punch.

"Yoru lands a direct hit on Sora!" shouted the commentator.

Yoru was about to lose consciousness before collapsing onto the ground. Sora remained standing for a moment as blood dripped from the corner of his mouth.

"So... you've gotten stronger. I didn't expect this." Sora said before collapsing as well.

Both Yoru and Sora fell onto the arena floor.

The entire arena suddenly went silent.

"Both contestants have lost consciousness!" the referee announced before beginning the count.

"1... 2... 3!"

"This match ends in a tie!" announced the commentator.

The crowd immediately began discussing the unbelievable fight.

"The match will continue once both contestants regain consciousness!" the commentator announced.

A team of healers, including Haru, rushed into the arena and carried the unconscious fighters to the medical room.


Medical Room

Kaito was already waiting as Haru treated Yoru's injuries.

"I didn't know my brother had become this strong." Kaito said quietly.

*But something about that fight felt unnatural...*

"What are you thinking about, Kaito?" Haru asked.

"There was something strange about Yoru during the fight. I can't explain it." Kaito replied while looking confused.

"I noticed it too." Haru answered seriously.

A few moments later, Yoru slowly opened his eyes.

"Did I win?" Yoru asked weakly.

"No. The match ended in a tie." Kaito replied.

"WHAT?!" Yoru shouted in shock.

Suddenly, the commentator's voice echoed through the hallway.

"If Yoru does not return to the arena within five minutes, he will be disqualified and Sora will be declared the winner!"

"You should go now." Haru said.

"Tell me something... how did you feel while fighting?" Kaito asked.

"I was at my limit... but at the same time, I felt stronger than ever before." Yoru replied while walking through the hallway.

Kaito suddenly turned around.

But there was nobody there.

*I feel like someone is watching us...*

"What happened, Kaito?" Yoru asked.

"Nothing." Kaito replied.


Back In Arena

"Since Yoru has not returned yet, the winner of this round is—"

The commentator suddenly stopped speaking.

A strange shadow slowly appeared from the other side of the arena entrance.

"Wait... someone's coming!" the commentator shouted.

The crowd immediately became excited.

"IT'S YORU!"

Everyone stared in shock as Yoru walked into the arena with a completely different presence.

"Are both contestants ready?" the referee asked.

"I thought I could defeat you easily." Sora said angrily.

"I won't lose that easily." Yoru replied confidently.

The match began once again.

However... something about Yoru had changed.

In the middle of the battle, one of Yoru's eyes suddenly turned silver.

"WHAT?! Yoru has awakened a new ability!" the commentator shouted in disbelief.

Sora stepped back in fear as pressure filled the entire arena.

The crowd was completely shocked.

"W-What is that...?" Sora whispered nervously.

Yoru's attack speed increased dramatically after awakening the silver eye. His movements became almost impossible to follow.

Despite the overwhelming power, Yoru's body trembled from exhaustion.

Yoru delivers a punch.

"Yoru' punch shattered all the windows in the arena and Sora dogded it."That punch sent shockwave through the arena and quakes went through the village.

"It seems Sora can no longer continue the fight!" the commentator announced.

"The winner of this round is YORU!"

The crowd erupted with excitement.

After being declared the winner, Yoru collapsed from exhaustion once again.

Everyone in the arena stared at Yoru in complete shock.

One silver eye fades away slowly.

And somewhere in the shadows... someone smiled.


END OF CHAPTER 2
